Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Dad is designed to help drivers manage documents, vehicle information, mileage, expenses and reminders.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may store:

  • Name and email address
  • Profile photo
  • Driver type and operating area
  • Vehicle information
  • Uploaded documents and document details
  • Insurance, MOT and licence information
  • Receipts and expense information
  • Mileage records
  • Saved parking location
  • Charge reminder information

How information is used

Information is used only to provide and improve the features of the app, including document storage, reminders, expense tracking, mileage tracking and driver organisation.

Document processing

The app may use on-device text recognition to read information from documents and receipts.

The current version of the app does not use generative AI to analyse uploaded documents.

Location

Location may be used when you choose features such as saving where your vehicle is parked or using Charge Guard.

The app is not designed to create a continuous history of your journeys.

Storage

Account information and uploaded content may be stored using secure cloud infrastructure used by the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as cloud hosting and storage providers.

Deleting your information

Users may delete individual documents and other stored information through the app where available.

Account deletion functionality will allow users to request deletion of their account and associated data.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information on our Support page.
