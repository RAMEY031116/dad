Your driving life, organised.

Dad is a simple companion app for UK drivers, helping you manage important documents, mileage, expenses, reminders and vehicle information in one place.

Designed with private-hire and professional drivers in mind, the app helps make paperwork easier to manage and keeps important information close when you need it.

Contact Support

What the app helps with

  • Store important driver and vehicle documents
  • Track insurance, MOT and licence expiry dates
  • Receive document reminders
  • Track mileage
  • Record fuel, parking, repairs and other expenses
  • Organise scanned receipts
  • Keep old documents archived
  • Share selected documents when needed
  • Keep vehicle information together

Privacy first

Your documents and account information are private to your account. The app is designed to collect only the information needed to provide its driver-management features.
